# Puzzle: Tool Shadow / Cork Board (working title)

**Status:** Theme HOT; this concept pass is exploratory — solution details not locked.  
**Zones:** Main (board + some tools) · Storage (more tools + Roman numeral legend) · Office (locked drawer + empty-board photo + number strip)  
**Related:** May feed Office access or an Office container; placement may feed a *different* puzzle's half.

---

## Concept as Described (v0)

1. Cork/shadow board in **Main** is missing tools.
2. Players find tools in **Main** and **Storage**.
3. In **Storage**, a wall note explains Roman numerals (I=1, IV=4, V=5, X=10, L=50, C=100, etc.).
4. Each tool has a **Roman numeral** embedded/engraved on it.
5. Players hang tools on the board.
6. In **Office**, a locked drawer has a **5-letter** lock.
7. Above the drawer: a **picture of the cork board with no tools** on it.
8. Below the drawer: the numbers **100 – 4 – 1 – 50**.
9. Convert via Romans → **C – IV – I – L** → word **CIVIL** → opens drawer.

### Russell's concern (valid)

**Where** the tools hang does not matter for CIVIL. You only need:
- the number strip (100-4-1-50), and
- Roman literacy (from the Storage chart — or prior knowledge).

The physical tools are almost optional for the cipher (except as flavor / teaching that Romans appear in-world). Hanging becomes busywork. **We should use correct placement for another payoff** (or make placement required to *read* or *reveal* the CIVIL path).

---

## Design Goal

Keep:
- Multi-zone tool hunt (Main + Storage)
- Roman legend in Storage
- Office drawer + CIVIL a-ha
- Back-and-forth / layered feel

Fix:
- Tool **placement** must do real work (unlock, reveal, or encode order)

---

## Options to Make Placement Matter

### Option A — Placement reveals the numbers (recommended split)

| Step | What happens |
|---|---|
| Hang tools on **correct** shadows | Board completes a circuit / magnets → **reveals or illuminates** the strip `100-4-1-50` (hidden panel, backlight, or stamp under a flap on the board itself — or unlocks a Main drawer that holds a photo of the numbers) |
| Storage Roman chart + revealed numbers | → CIVIL |
| Office | Empty-board photo is a **reminder** "go finish the board" / shows which silhouettes exist — not the cipher itself |

**Why it works:** Wrong hang = no numbers. Placement is mandatory. CIVIL stays a nice Office a-ha once numbers exist. Tools' engraved Romans become optional confirmation ("yes, C and IV exist") or can be removed if redundant.

### Option B — Placement *is* the word (order from the Office photo)

| Step | What happens |
|---|---|
| Office photo of empty board | Silhouettes are marked or ordered (e.g. faint 1–4, or left-to-right reading order matching the photo's crop) |
| Tools have Romans (C, IV, I, L, and distractors) | Hang each on its correct shadow |
| Read in photo order | C-IV-I-L → CIVIL |
| Number strip `100-4-1-50` | Hint / confirmation for players who don't "see" the word yet — or drop the strip if placement+Romans are enough |

**Why it works:** Wrong tool on wrong shadow = wrong word. Placement matters. Risk: 5-letter lock vs C-IV-I-L is 4 glyph groups → **CIVIL is 5 letters** (C-I-V-I-L) if you expand IV as I+V, or 4 chunks if IV stays one symbol. **Need to settle encoding:** either lock accepts CIVIL (5 letters) from C+IV+I+L with IV→"IV" as two letters I+V, or use five separate Romans (e.g. C-I-V-I-L as five tools). Flag for later.

### Option C — Two outputs from one board (placement ≠ CIVIL)

| Output | Source |
|---|---|
| **Output 1 — Placement** | Correct hang → unlocks Storage→Office gate, or gives **half** of trunk lock, or powers fuse/compressor path |
| **Output 2 — Romans / CIVIL** | Tool engravings + Storage chart + Office number strip → CIVIL drawer — placement irrelevant for this half |

**Why it works:** Matches Russell's note ("use tool placement for another part of another puzzle"). Board becomes a multi-layer hub. CIVIL can stay almost as designed.

### Option D — Placement gates reading the Romans

Romans are on the **backs** of tools or under grease; only when seated on the correct hook do they face a mirror / window / peephole that lets you read the sequence in order. Placement required to *see* C-IV-I-L.

---

## Current Lean — Option C (not locked)

Russell leaning **C** (2026-08-06): correct tool **placement** feeds a *different* puzzle payoff; **CIVIL** stays a separate knowledge/cipher beat (tools’ Romans + Storage chart + Office `100-4-1-50`).

**Why C is attractive:** the cork board can stay **fully mechanical / non-electronic** — hang tools for a physical check (GM honor, mechanical latch, or “obvious completeness” that reveals a hidden non-powered thing). No magnets/RFID required for v1. Simpler build.

**Still to decide when ready:**
- What does correct placement actually *give* (Office key half, trunk part, unlocks a Main container, reveals a non-electronic clue card behind a tool, etc.)?
- Distractor tools?
- CIVIL encoding (C-IV-I-L → five-letter CIVIL) finalized

Avoid pure v0 (hang does nothing). A remains a backup if we later want one chained reveal.

Also fix **CIVIL encoding** explicitly later:
- Preferred clean version: **five tools** engraved **C, I, V, I, L** (two different "I" tools or I appearing twice via position), OR
- Four tools **C, IV, I, L** and the lock clue teaches "IV = letters I+V" via the number strip 100-4-1-50 (4→IV→I and V as two letters). The strip already suggests 4 → IV as one Roman token that becomes two letters when spelling CIVIL.
