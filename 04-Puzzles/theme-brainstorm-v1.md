## Air Compressor — Alternate Uses (brainstorm, not chosen)

Keep the compressor as a presence/prop; ditch or demote "set needle to PSI → latch" as the only trick:

| Alt use | Player does | Feel |
|---|---|---|
| **A. Air tools power gate** | Compressor must be "on" + hose clicked into impact wrench / blow gun before that tool works on another puzzle (e.g. lug nuts won't budge until air is connected) | Layers #2 or #1 with compressor |
| **B. Blow-out reveal** | Use blow gun to clear sawdust/grease covering a stamped code or wall marking | Discovery, not a lock |
| **C. Pneumatic tube / parts runner** | Send a small capsule/part through a shop air tube to Storage or Office | Magical + thematic |
| **D. Whistle / tone** | Air through different fittings produces tones; match a sequence (like pipes) | Audio puzzle |
| **E. Inflate to shape** | Inflate a bladder/jack bag under something to lift a plate (not a full car lift) | Physical, different from #7 |
| **F. Hose routing** | Plug hose to the correct quick-connect on the wall (color/size) to power remote latch | More "plumbing" than PSI reading |

Flag favorite(s) when ready — compressor can support a HOT puzzle rather than be a weak standalone.

## Detail Cards (HOT + open only)

### 1. Tool Shadow Board — HOT → concept in progress

### 4. Air Compressor — RETHINK

### 5. Engine Timing Marks — HOT
Rotate crank/cam until marks align. Layer: Storage diagram + Office TDC ref.

### 6. Spark Plug Firing Order — HOT
Plug wires to cylinders. Layer: incomplete sticker + Office invoice digit.

### 8. Parts Catalog / Bin Wall — HOT
Look up part number; pull correct Storage bin. Strong Storage exploration beat.

### 10. Fake OBD / Blink Codes — HOT
Blink pattern or printout → Office legend → code entry or "replace this part."

### 11. Wheel Alignment Dials — HOT
Set dials to wall/printout specs → green lights / drawer.

### 12. Paint Color Chips / Mix — HOT
Match panel code / mix ratio. Layer across Main car + Storage/Office.

### 13. Jumper Cables — UNRATED
Clamp polarity to power a circuit. Thematic cousin to Sparky battery (keep separate).

### 14. H-Pattern Shifter Maze — HOT
Physical H-gate path → cubby/glovebox. Layer: pattern on Storage wall.

### 15. Brake Bleeding Order — HOT
Open bleeders in order + pump pedal. Layer: manual in Office + problem corner in Main.

### 16. Wall Meta — HOT
Storage wall + Office wall must be combined (digits, shapes, sightline). Pure back-and-forth layer pattern.

### 17. Path-Tracing Cogs — HOT → concept in progress
See [`02-path-tracing-cogs/puzzle-concept.md`](./02-path-tracing-cogs/puzzle-concept.md).

Four colored pegs (red / black / yellow / green) on a pegboard. Four transparent colored **cogs**, each with a pathway segment. Stack on matching pegs (order irrelevant); rotate so paths meet. **Trace one continuous line** through letters — path only completes with all four cogs (e.g. get STA early; RR/Y need the later pieces).

**Distribution (Russell):** three find spots — cog 1, then cog 2, then **cogs 3+4 together**. Two cogs alone are not guessable.
