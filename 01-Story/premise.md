# Premise: Tony's Garage

## Logline

You just inherited **Tony's Garage** from your late Uncle Tony, an automotive shop you know nothing about. Your first day on the job unravels what looks like a mob operation hiding behind an auto shop — dirty money, coded recordings about "the Don," photos of people "taken care of," a Chief of Police shaking hands with Tony — only to reveal Tony wasn't a criminal at all. He was secretly funding free, anonymous car repairs for people at rock bottom, with the Chief of Police as his partner in the operation. The final twist: will *you* carry on "the family" business?

## Theme Angle

This is the "**looks like monkey business, isn't**" angle — suspicious behavior that's actually a good deed done in secret. That's a nice thematic tie-in to the overall project name (*Monkey Business*) worth keeping in mind as we design puzzle flavor and signage.

**Decided:** the mascot — the "dead thing in the trunk" — is a **monkey holding a wrench**, tying "grease monkey" mechanic slang directly to the overall *Monkey Business* brand. His nametag reads **Sparky**. See [`characters.md`](./characters.md).

## Tone

Mystery/thriller investigation tone for most of the room (tense, suspicious, "what did I get myself into"), pivoting hard into warmth/heart at the reveal, ending on an emotional, uplifting note. Not a comedy — the humor (if any) is dry/gruff, mostly from the Manager character.

## Core Emotional Beat

Fear/suspicion → doubt → reframing of evidence → realization of a hidden goodness → personal choice to accept responsibility (or not).

## Central Question Driving the Room

*"What did I actually inherit?"* — Players **assume** "something shady / mob-adjacent." The room never states that. The real answer is "a responsibility to secretly help people." The a-ha is realizing every clue told a different story than the one they invented. See [`../03-Flow/suspicion-curve.md`](../03-Flow/suspicion-curve.md).

## Branching Resolution

The game has **two valid endings**, chosen by the player at the very end (YES/NO button press), not a fail state:

- **YES** — accept joining "the family": full light/music finale, "...IS FOR FAMILY" sign completes, photos of people helped illuminate.
- **NO** — decline: quieter, still warm, Tony reassigns the role to the Manager.

Both are "wins" — this is a values choice, not a puzzle failure. See [`narrative-arc.md`](./narrative-arc.md) for full beat breakdown.
